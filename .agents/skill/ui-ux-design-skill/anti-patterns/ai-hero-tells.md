# Anti-Pattern: AI Landing Hero Tells

> **Evidence Classification:**
> - `DIRECT`: [`de-ai-landing-hero`](https://www.designmotionhq.com/patterns/de-ai-landing-hero).

---

## 1. Description & The 5 Verified Tells
Landing pages generated by AI tools follow a statistically predictable, low-intent visual formula. They decorate heavily because they lack concrete product details.

### The 5 Verified Tells
1. **The Gradient Headline of Adjectives:**
   - *Example:* *"Supercharge your seamless workflow with intelligent, next-generation AI."*
   - *Flaw:* The model sells buzzwords because it does not know what the software actually does.
2. **The Ambient Purple Blob:**
   - *Example:* A blurry radial neon gradient or glowing sphere floating behind cards.
   - *Flaw:* Added to inject synthetic visual energy into a layout that lacks functional structure.
3. **The 50/50 Button Split:**
   - *Example:* "Get Started" and "Learn More" rendered side-by-side as identical filled buttons.
   - *Flaw:* Splits user attention equally instead of driving the primary conversion path.
4. **Fake Social Proof:**
   - *Example:* *"Trusted by 10,000+ users"* above 5 unrecognizable gray placeholder icons.
   - *Flaw:* Unverifiable metrics and anonymous logos destroy credibility.
5. **The 3-Card Feature Row:**
   - *Example:* Three cards with a Lucide icon inside a pastel circle and 1-word headers ("Fast", "Secure", "Easy").
   - *Flaw:* Present on virtually every generated template; communicates zero concrete product capability.

---

## 2. The Meta-Rule
> *"The generated hero decorates because it has nothing concrete to show. Swap each decoration for the product itself and the AI look disappears."* — DesignMotionHQ

---

## 3. The Refactoring Protocol

| AI Tell | Senior Designer Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Adjective Headline** | Plain text naming the exact **outcome** and **audience**: *"Close the month in one afternoon — Bookkeeping for teams under 20 without spreadsheets."* |
| **Purple Blob** | Neutral canvas base (`#121212` or clean off-white). Reserve accent color **strictly** for the primary CTA and real product screenshot. |
| **50/50 Buttons** | **90/10 weight ratio:** 1 filled primary button + 1 text link with a trailing arrow (`→`). |
| **Fake Social Proof** | **One real customer quote:** A real name, title/company, and a result with a unit (e.g. *"reduced onboarding from 4 days to 20 minutes"*). |
| **3 Feature Cards** | **One actual product screenshot** accompanied by 3 callout annotations pointing at real interactive features. |
